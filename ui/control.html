🎩 How Smoke & Mirrors works

The idea

Keep your projector in normal extend mode. While this app runs, the projector shows a cover (curtain, image, GIF, video, countdown, black…) and your audience never sees you fiddling with windows, logins or slides.

Set everything up behind the cover, check it in the monitors, then hit TAKE. Like a TV control room: PREVIEW is what's next, PROGRAM is what the audience sees.

Typical workflows

  1. Reveal: drag your slideshow fullscreen onto the projector (it stays hidden behind the cover), preview “Reveal desktop”, press Space. The cover fades away and your real projector screen shows — full quality, presenter view works as usual.
  2. Peek: add a Window / Screen source of that slideshow window and select it as “peek” on the Reveal scene to see what's behind the curtain in the monitors.
  3. Mirror: add a window as a source and TAKE it to show just that window on the projector — your notifications and other windows stay private.

Safety

The app never changes display settings and installs nothing. If it closes, crashes or is killed, its window vanishes and the projector shows your desktop exactly as it would without the app. Unplugging the projector closes the output immediately. If a mirrored source disappears while live, the projector falls back to your cover.

Scene tips

  • ★ marks the cover — shown when the app starts and as safety fallback.
  • Test pattern helps focus and align the beamer and shows its real resolution.
  • Countdown starts when it goes live.
  • Edits in the inspector only touch the preview. Press TAKE to push them live.
